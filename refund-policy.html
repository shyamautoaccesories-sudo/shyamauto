Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

7-day easy returns

We offer a 7-day return window from the date of delivery on most items, provided the product is unused, in its original packaging, and with all tags/accessories intact.

Non-returnable items

  • Used or installed items (e.g. fitted LED lights, opened intercoms)
  • Items damaged due to misuse after delivery
  • Products explicitly marked "non-returnable" on the product page

How to request a return

Contact us via the Contact/Support page or WhatsApp with your order number and reason for return. Our team will arrange a pickup or share return instructions.

Refunds

Once the returned item is received and inspected, refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. For online payments made via Razorpay, the refund is credited back to the same payment method (UPI/Card/Netbanking) used at checkout. For COD orders, refunds are processed via bank transfer. Exchanges are also available for size/fit issues on eligible items like helmets and jackets.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped by contacting our support team. Once an order is shipped, it cannot be cancelled but can be returned as per the policy above after delivery.
