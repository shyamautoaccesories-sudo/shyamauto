About Us

Shyam Auto is a Patna-based motorbike accessories and gear store, stocking genuine helmets, riding gloves, jackets, LED lighting, and everyday bike accessories from trusted brands.

We started as a neighbourhood shop on Bailey Road and have grown into an online store so riders across Bihar can order genuine gear with confidence — no local market duplicates, no guesswork on fitting.
